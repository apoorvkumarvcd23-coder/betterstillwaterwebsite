SHARAN

SHARAN (Sanctuary for Health and Reconnection to Animals and Nature) is a Nari Shakti Award–winning organisation that has spent years helping people reclaim their health through a whole-food, plant-based way of living.

SHARAN is especially well known for diabetes care — their approach has helped many people manage and even reverse type 2 diabetes by addressing its root causes through food and lifestyle, often reducing reliance on medication under medical guidance. Rather than quick fixes, they focus on sustainable change: the kind of everyday nourishment, knowledge, and community that supports long-term wellbeing for diabetes, heart health, weight, and more.
