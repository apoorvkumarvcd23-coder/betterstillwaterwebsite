Meet Aria

A companion that walks with you.

Hi, I'm Aria.

I'm Stilwater's AI companion — here to help you settle into a plant-based, whole-food lifestyle for healthier, calmer living.

I suggest delicious plant-based recipes you can ease into based on your taste and meal preferences — no overnight overhaul, just steady, enjoyable change.

I help you keep a simple daily journal of what you eat, how you move, and how you meditate — so you can actually see the rhythm of your practice.

And if you start drifting from your plan, I'll give you a gentle call to check in and help you find your way back. Not a notification. A real, kind nudge.

I also answer your questions about wellness and nutrition — and when something's beyond me, I connect you to verified providers and doctors, so you always know where to turn.
