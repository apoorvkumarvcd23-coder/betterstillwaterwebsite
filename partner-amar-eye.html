About Amar Eye Yoga

Amar Eye Yoga is a Pondicherry-based natural eye care clinic focused on eye yoga, natural eye exercises, lifestyle guidance, and supportive dietary practices. The centre presents its approach as a non-surgical, holistic model for people seeking natural support for eye strain, weak eyesight, screen-related discomfort, and other eye-health concerns.

The clinic describes its work as a combination of natural eye exercises, Dr. Bates method-inspired practices, Ayurvedic herbal traditions, yogic therapy, diet, and meditation. Its care pathway begins with appointment booking and eye check-up, followed by customized treatment guidance based on the individual’s eye condition.

Treatment Philosophy

Amar Eye Yoga’s philosophy is built around the belief that the body has an inherent ability to rejuvenate when supported with the right combination of exercise, diet, therapy, and mental focus. Their model positions eye care as part of a broader lifestyle discipline rather than a purely symptom-focused intervention.

Process

  1. Book an appointment by phone or email.
  2. Conduct an eye check-up and understand the condition.
  3. Begin customized natural eye care guidance.
  4. Continue prescribed exercises, diet practices, and follow-up support.
